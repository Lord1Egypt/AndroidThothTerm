First release of ThothTerm Trixie.

* Debian 13 "trixie" for ARM64, with apt, dpkg and Debian's own sudo
* Package sources: trixie, trixie-updates and trixie-security, main only
* Multiple windows, extra keys, pinch-to-zoom, Arabic and other RTL text
* LAN Mode (off by default): a paired browser on your Wi-Fi gets its own terminal
* The F-Droid build downloads the Debian GNU/Linux 13 (trixie) environment
  only after you agree,
  and checks its size and SHA-256 first
