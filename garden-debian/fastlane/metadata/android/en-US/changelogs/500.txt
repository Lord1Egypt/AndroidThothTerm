ThothDock 0.3.0 engine: Flatpak and snap. Debian's flatpak installs and runs apps, its sandboxes run by ThothDock. A snap command installs from the Snap Store and runs apps and services. Each app gets its own loopback address. A new Apps screen and Web Panel tab list them: run, stop, remove. The terminal shows 24-bit colours; the welcome banner no longer draws on a 0x0 screen. docker rmi of a repository's last tag removes its digest references.
