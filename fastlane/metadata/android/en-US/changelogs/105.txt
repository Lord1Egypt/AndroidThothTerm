A visual release. The terminal itself is unchanged from 0.1.4.

ThothTerm Ubuntu has its own launcher identity: a terminal cup on the Garden
petals. It is a proper adaptive icon, so the artwork and its background are
separate layers and every launcher shape -- circle, squircle, rounded square --
crops padding rather than petals. A monochrome version is included, so Android's
themed icons follow your wallpaper colours instead of falling back to the old
mark. The legacy icons were regenerated to match for every screen density.

Starting the app now shows the round eight-petal emblem, centred and at its own
proportions in portrait and landscape alike, through the Android 12 splash
screen API rather than a stretched full-screen image.

The master artwork and the steps needed to regenerate these assets are kept in
the repository under docs/branding, so the identity can be rebuilt exactly.
