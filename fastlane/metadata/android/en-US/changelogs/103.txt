Three terminal fixes.

Text selection now starts anywhere on a line. The navigation drawer claimed the
left edge of the window, and touching there peeked the drawer open and
cancelled the touch, so a long press on the first few columns never became a
selection. The drawer is opened from the toolbar button instead.

The keyboard no longer gets stuck. Volume Up and Volume Down act as the Fn and
Ctrl modifiers, and pressing one twice latched it on. That state lived with the
session rather than the view, so it survived backgrounding the app and even
"Restart terminal" -- ordinary letters kept coming out as escape sequences until
the process was force stopped. Modifiers are now cleared whenever a session is
paused or resumed, and by "Restart terminal".

Scrollback can be cleared without restarting the session. The terminal now
implements CSI 3 J (erase saved lines), and a new "Clear scrollback" menu item
runs it directly. Whether the shell's own "clear" command also erases scrollback
depends on the terminfo entry for the selected TERM; the default, screen, does
not ask for it.
