R8 is now enabled for release builds. Nothing else changed from 0.1.6.

Release builds are shrunk, optimized and obfuscated with the platform's standard
optimized configuration. The download is smaller: the F-Droid build drops from
5.9 MB to 3.3 MB.

The terminal's native bridge needs its names kept, because the native library
registers its methods against class and method names written out as literals
rather than through the Java_* naming convention. Exactly those two classes and
their six native methods are pinned; everything else in the app, including their
own outer classes, is fully optimized. The minified build was tested on a
physical Android 16 device before release.

No terminal, clipboard, lifecycle, service, branding or runtime behaviour
changed.
