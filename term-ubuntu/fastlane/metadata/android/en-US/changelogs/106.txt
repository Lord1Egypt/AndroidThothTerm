The branding release. The terminal itself is unchanged from 0.1.5.

0.1.5 gave the app its launcher identity and its Android startup splash, but two
screens inside the app were still drawing the older hand-drawn mark: the
first-run screen that extracts Ubuntu, and the navigation drawer header. Both now
show the same round eight-petal emblem as the startup splash, at their own size
and proportions.

The rule is now consistent everywhere. Outside the app -- the launcher icon and
any shortcut you create -- carries the terminal cup. Inside the app, every place
that shows the app's own identity carries the round emblem. Functional icons are
untouched.

No terminal, clipboard, lifecycle, service or runtime behaviour changed.
