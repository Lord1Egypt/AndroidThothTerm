Clipboard fix, plus a full regression pass over the 0.1.3 fixes.

Text copied in other apps can now be pasted into the terminal. Paste only
accepted clips the system described as exactly text/plain, but apps that copy
styled text publish it as text/html -- measured on device, text copied from
Telegram arrives as text/html even when it carries no formatting, so Paste was
greyed out and did nothing. Any textual clip is accepted now, and the plain
form is pasted rather than the markup. Clips that hold only a URI or an Intent
are still refused: Paste is for text, and the terminal will not follow a link
to find some.

Copying from the terminal is unchanged, and the floating selection toolbar and
the paste shortcut use the same path.

Re-verified on a physical device with no regressions: text selection at the
start of a line, modifier state after backgrounding, Clear scrollback and
CSI 3 J, Exit, and multiple sessions.
